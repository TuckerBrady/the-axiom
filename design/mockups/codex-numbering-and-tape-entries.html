1 · THE NUMBERING SCHEME — pick one

Today the on-screen ENTRY 00X number comes from a static array index, and COGS's spoken counts contradict themselves (source = "first entry" / terminal = "two entries", but gear = "two entries in two missions", configNode = "three entries"). Once tapes join the Codex, every milestone count has to be re-derived anyway. The fix: entry number = the order COGS catalogues it (already tracked in codexStore.discoveredIds). Two ways to count it:

My recommendation: Scheme A. It's the most literal reading of "numbered in the order discovered," it makes COGS's "first entry / second entry" lines on Source and Terminal correct as written, and the player never has to wonder why an entry is unnumbered. Either way, the milestone counts in COGS's reveal lines get rewritten to match (see §2).
