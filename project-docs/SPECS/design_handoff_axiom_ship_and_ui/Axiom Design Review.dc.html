THE COLOR PROBLEM
The color prop is documented as the category signal — blue Physics, purple Protocol — but every icon hardcodes its own accents on top. Count how many jobs each hue is doing:
Physics beam · Source · conveyor start pulley · Config gate strips · Latch write arrow · Merger centre · Splitter magnet 0
Terminal · gate PASS · conveyor end pulley · Splitter magnet 1 · Bridge vertical path · locked-piece border
Protocol beam · HUD chrome · Scanner crosshair · Inverter bubble · Counter ring · Latch read arrow
Amber on a piece means seven different things. So a player cannot learn "amber = the physical signal" — the rule they need before Kepler Belt. Fix: reserve amber and cyan exclusively for the two beams, and let every static piece accent be copper or lavender. Category then survives at 22pt because it is the only saturated thing on the icon.

HUD STACK — 5 LINES
The centre column stacks sector / level id / title / timer / pulse. Four of the five are 7–9pt. iOS minimum for sustained reading is 11pt. The pulse counter is #1A3050 on #06090f — 1.5:1 contrast, effectively invisible. The sector tag is 2.4:1 at 7pt.
HUD COMPETES WITH THE BOARD
Level title at Orbitron 14 bold is the loudest text on screen, but it is the one thing the player never needs mid-puzzle. The timer and pulse count — the things that change — are the quietest.
BOARD MATH IS SOUND
61pt cells, 34pt icons. Above the 44pt touch floor with room. The board is not the problem — what sits inside the cells is.
TRAY IS THE BOTTLENECK
A 56pt cell holding a 22pt icon, an 8pt count badge and a 7pt price. Three information layers in 56pt. Icon should be 32pt minimum, price moved to a long-press or the requisition panel where the buying decision actually happens.
1c The ship — three ships
You have three unrelated Axioms in the repo. Before anyone redraws anything, one has to be declared canon.
SIDE PROFILE
design/screens/

Faces right. Twin dorsal nacelles, chin guns, dorsal turret, comm tower. Most characterful of the three — and the only one with weld scars and a painted-over hull marking, which is the story.
TOP-DOWN

AxiomShip.tsx
Different hull, two nacelles aft, eight system lights in a row. Uses #38BDF8/#B87333 — not your tokens. Reads as a schematic, not a vessel.
THREE-QUARTER
components/
ShipRepairProgress.tsx
A fourth hull shape again, faces right, eight repair zones mapped to A1-1…A1-8. Mechanically the most valuable — this is the one the player earns.
RECOMMENDATION
Make the three-quarter repair view canon and derive the others from it. It is the only rendering that carries game state, it is the one on screen most often, and "the ship lights up as you fix it" is the emotional payload of the whole Axiom sector. The side profile becomes a Codex illustration of the same silhouette; the hub gets the same three-quarter view at a smaller size rather than a second geometry.
Second, and more urgent than the silhouette: the ship is drawn almost entirely below 0.4 alpha. Unrepaired zones sit at 0.01–0.2 stroke opacity — on an OLED phone at 40% brightness, outdoors, most of the vessel is literally not there. The repair reveal has nowhere to travel from, because the "before" state is invisible rather than dim. Floor unrepaired strokes at 0.25 and lift repaired to 0.85, so the arc is dark-but-present → lit.
Third: ship-axiom.html has a malformed element — the pylon accent polygon is written points="132" y1="90" x2="140"…, mixing polygon and line attributes. It renders nothing. Whoever approved that prototype approved a ship with a missing part.
I can spec the canon and the treatment, but I should be straight with you: I can't produce a finished ship illustration. Hand-built SVG at this level of detail is not something I do well, and I can't generate images. What the ship needs is either a real illustrator or one of your three existing drawings promoted and cleaned up. My recommendation is the latter.
